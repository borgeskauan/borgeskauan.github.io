Instant Payment System

{{"I built a system to move money between simulated banks. I wanted to understand what it would take to keep payments fast and correct as demand increased." | t}}

{{"I was responsible for the design, development and testing, including the tools used to evaluate it." | t}}

{{"Setting the target" | t}}

{{"I focused on payments between banks and set a target: at least 2,000 payment attempts per second, with 99% of final answers reaching the bank within one second." | t}}

{{"The tests also included payments declined for insufficient funds and the same payment being sent more than once. The system needed to handle those correctly while keeping up with demand." | t}}

{{"Finding what needed to change" | t}}

{{"As I increased demand, I investigated where delays came from. Some pauses came from my own testing tool. I rebuilt it so I could test the payment system under steadier demand." | t}}

{{"I also found that making one part faster didn’t necessarily improve the complete payment. I measured the complete journey, through to the final answer reaching the bank. That helped me decide which changes to keep." | t}}

{{"What the tests showed" | t}}

{{"The final system met the target in two separate 15-minute tests." | t}}
