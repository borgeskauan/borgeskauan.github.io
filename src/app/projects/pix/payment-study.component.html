Instant Payment System

{{"I built a system to move money between simulated banks. My focus was how it behaved when thousands of payments arrived every second." | t}}

{{"I owned the design, implementation and testing, including the tools used to measure it." | t}}

{{"The target I set" | t}}

{{"At least 2,000 new payments a second, with 99% of final answers reaching the bank within one second." | t}}

{{"How I evaluated the system" | t}}

{{"Speed mattered only if the bank also got the right answer. I counted a payment as complete when its final confirmation arrived, and tested completed payments alongside declines and repeated attempts." | t}}

{{"To reach the target, I measured where the system spent time, revised the design and tested again. Both final runs met the target. I kept the slower result too, so the evidence shows the variation I observed." | t}}

{{"Check the measurements" | t}}

{{"Both runs used the same code and settings. The test checked the lowest activity in any one-second window as well as the average, so a later burst could not hide a quiet period." | t}}
