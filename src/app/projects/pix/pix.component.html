Instant Payment System

{{"I built a payment system inspired by Pix to handle thousands of payments every second while keeping the results correct and the banks informed." | t}}

{{"I was responsible for its design, implementation and testing. The project uses simulated banks and runs on a single computer." | t}}

{{"The challenge was to keep up during busy periods without losing track of any payment. If a bank sent the same payment again while waiting for an answer, it still needed to receive one clear result, without moving money twice." | t}}

{{"I set a target of at least 2,000 new payments per second, with 99% of final answers reaching the sending bank within one second. I built the tools to keep that demand steady and check the answers as they arrived." | t}}

{{"Each round of testing showed where time was being spent. I used that evidence to change the design and repeated the tests. The measurement ended only when the bank received the final result." | t}}
