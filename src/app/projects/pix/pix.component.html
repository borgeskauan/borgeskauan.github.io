Instant Payment System

{{"I designed, built and tested a system that coordinates payments between simulated banks. It moves the agreed amount and tells the sending bank whether the payment completed or was declined." | t}}

{{"Repository" | t}} ↗ {{"Demo & setup" | t}} ↗

{{"What I wanted to find out" | t}}

{{"Inspired by Pix, I wanted to handle at least 2,000 new payments per second, return 99% of answers within one second, and check that the results stayed correct. The whole system had to run on my local computer." | t}}

{{"A payment through the system" | t}}

{{"One bank wants to transfer R$100 to another. My system coordinates what happens between them." | t}}

{{"The core handles the money banks hold for transfers between them. Customer accounts are represented separately in the demo." | t}}

{{"A payment can be repeated, compete with another payment, or finish before its confirmation arrives. Each situation changes what the system needs to remember." | t}}
